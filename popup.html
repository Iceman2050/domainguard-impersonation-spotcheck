      Why am I seeing this?

      Phishing sites impersonate brands you trust by registering lookalike domains: one letter off, lookalike foreign characters, extra words like "secure-login", or the brand name hidden in a subdomain of a domain they own.

      This extension checks the current site's domain against those patterns entirely on your device. Nothing about your browsing is sent anywhere.

      It catches common impersonation shapes — not targeted attacks on clean domains, and it can't confirm who really owns a domain. When in doubt, type the address yourself instead of clicking links.
